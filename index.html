About

Hello there! I'm Joe and I love maps.

I am committed to exploring new ways of using visualization design principles and cartographic best practices to help derive meaningful insight from geographic information.

My work ranges from thematic maps of European statistics to 3D-looking relief maps, and I build open-source JavaScript tools for mapping and visualizing gridded data. I also work as a consultant – get in touch if you have a mapping project in mind.
